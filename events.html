Events

List of currently available events for seniors to partake in

Activity Description
Health & Wellness
Virtual Exercise Classes Chair yoga, stretching, and low-impact aerobics are examples of online exercise programs. accessible live or on demand to promote flexibility and physical wellness at the senior's own pace.
Cooking and Nutrition Webinars Classes on healthy cooking that include meal planning, senior nutrition recipes and advice, and simple culinary demonstrations to encourage healthier eating and overall wellbeing.
Health and Wellness Webinars Health specialists lead sessions on mental health, preventive care, and managing chronic diseases to educate and empower seniors with useful guidance.
Learning & Personal Development
Book Club A virtual reading group with monthly book selections and video discussions.
Tech Help Sessions Basic digital training sessions, teaching seniors how to use smartphones, computers and other digital devices
Local History and Culture Tours local guided tours of museums and historical sites.
Creative & Social Engagement
Gardening Workshops Classes on gardening methods such as container planting and herb growing can be held indoors or outdoors.
Art and Craft Classes Creative workshops including painting, knitting, or scrapbooking.
Volunteer Match Program connects elderly people with neighborhood volunteers for housework, errands, or company. Builds meaningful relationships.
Outdoor Camping Experiences Organized day trips or overnight camping tailored to seniors. Activities include nature walks, birdwatching, and storytelling for outdoor enjoyment and social bonding.
